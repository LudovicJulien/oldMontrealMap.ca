Everything in the historic quarter, on one fold

Download or print the official map of Old Montréal before your visit. No app needed. It's the same map you'll find at every hotel desk and tourist centre in the quarter.

Save to your phone

Open the PDF above and tap the download icon in your browser. The map works offline, so no signal is needed while you walk the quarter. Works on iOS and Android.

Print at home

Print on a single letter or A4 sheet, double-sided and folded in thirds. That's the same format as the physical map. Landscape orientation gives the best result.

Pick up the paper version free

Prefer paper? The printed map is available free at hotel concierge desks, the Tourist Information Centre at Place Jacques-Cartier, Bonsecours Market and the Montreal Convention Centre.
